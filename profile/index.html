Who we are

One accountable team, around the clock.

Enjazat Al Mamlakah for General Contracting was founded in Makkah in 2007. We finish the spaces where the Kingdom works and gathers, protect buildings against its harshest hours, and keep their systems running through the night, under one contract and one promise.

2007Founded in Makkah
3Specialities, one team
ISO9001 · 14001 · 45001
Workplace lounge · design proposal
Vision

To be the Saudi partner that builds, enhances and sustains the places where the Kingdom works, lives and welcomes the world, so that every building we touch serves its owner longer, runs more efficiently and is ready for Vision 2030.

Mission

From Makkah since 2007, we make buildings better places to work, live and visit. Our specialities deliver as one accountable team: approved details before we start, systems that save energy and water, tests before handover, and maintenance long after it.
